What the score means

Research fit is a weighted score from 0–100 that summarizes historical market behavior under your selected lens. It is not a probability, a valuation, a forecast, or a recommendation to buy.

Scores are absolute transforms, not percentiles. They do not improve merely because another asset is removed. Assets without sufficient data receive no score.

Active weights

Fixed factor transforms (clipped to 0–100)

Momentum = 50 + (30d return / 30) × 50
Relative = 50 + (30d return minus BTC 30d return) / 15 × 50
Stability = 100 − annualized 30d volatility / 150 × 100
Drawdown = 100 − maximum 90d drawdown / 50 × 100
Activity = (log10(estimated USD volume) − 6) / 3 × 100

These bounds and weights are research heuristics chosen for this app. They have not been backtested or validated for investment performance. “Lower volatility” is relative to the available crypto assets, not a claim of low financial risk.

Calculation details

7d and 30d returns compare the latest completed daily close with the close exactly 7 or 30 calendar days earlier. Bitcoin-relative return is the difference in percentage points over matching dates.

Volatility uses the sample standard deviation of 30 daily log returns × √365. Maximum drawdown is the largest close-to-prior-peak decline across 90 consecutive daily closes. All required metrics must be available to score an asset.

Estimated daily USD activity = 24h base-asset trading volume × last price. It is an activity proxy, not order-book depth or a guarantee of executable liquidity.

Due diligence still to do

The app does not verify token supply schedules, unlocks, holder concentration, issuer financials, protocol exploits, contract permissions, or regulatory status. Record those checks and their sources in your thesis before making a decision.

Public price movements cannot establish fraud or account abuse. Past performance and a high research-fit score do not establish future returns.
